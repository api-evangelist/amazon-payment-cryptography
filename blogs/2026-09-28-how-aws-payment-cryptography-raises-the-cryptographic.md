In this blog, learn how on-prem HSMs can be replaced by AWS Payment Cryptography which is a fully managed, cloud-native payment cryptographic service designed to help customers meet PCI PIN Security, PCI P2PE, PCI DSS requirements and regional requirements such as Cartes Bancaires, France's national card payment network and Australia Standard 2805 (AS2805).
